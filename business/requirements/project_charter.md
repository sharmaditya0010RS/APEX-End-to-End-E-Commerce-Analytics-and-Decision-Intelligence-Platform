# PROJECT APEX — PROJECT CHARTER

## Project Identity
Project: Enterprise E-Commerce Revenue, Customer Intelligence and Operational Excellence Platform
Project Type: End-to-End Analytics and Decision Intelligence
Source: Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset
Business Scenario: Simulated marketplace leadership engagement
Currency: Brazilian Real (BRL)

## Business Problem
Marketplace leadership lacks a unified, validated view of commercial performance, customer behavior, delivery reliability, seller operations and business improvement opportunities.

## Mission
Transform historical marketplace transactions into a reproducible analytical system supporting evidence-based operational and commercial decisions.

## Objectives
O1: Establish a governed commercial KPI framework.
O2: Identify measurable revenue, customer and seller patterns.
O3: Investigate delivery performance and customer satisfaction.
O4: Deliver 10 connected executive and functional Power BI pages.
O5: Develop carefully validated predictive analysis.
O6: Quantify strategic opportunities through transparent scenarios.
O7: Deliver a documented, tested and reproducible analytics platform.

## In Scope
Real Olist transactional data
Data profiling and quality control
PostgreSQL and dbt modeling
SQL and Python analytics
Statistical analysis
Power BI semantic modeling
Interactive executive reporting
Forecasting and predictive modeling
Business recommendations
Automated verification
Executive presentation

## Out of Scope
Live Olist production integrations
Real-time 2026 marketplace reporting
Actual company profit calculation without cost data
Verified marketing attribution without campaign data
Realized savings without implemented interventions
Production customer-facing decision automation

## Success Criteria
All material KPIs have clear definitions and grain.
Critical KPI results reconcile between PostgreSQL and Power BI.
All required source relationships are validated.
Every dashboard has a documented stakeholder decision.
All significant findings have traceable analytical evidence.
Predictive performance is evaluated on temporally held-out observations.
No future information leaks into predictive features.
The solution can be reconstructed from documented setup procedures.
Financial scenarios distinguish assumptions from observed data.

## Delivery Model
Ten phases, numbered 0 through 9.
One phase completed per conversation turn.
Git checkpoints and validation evidence at every phase.
