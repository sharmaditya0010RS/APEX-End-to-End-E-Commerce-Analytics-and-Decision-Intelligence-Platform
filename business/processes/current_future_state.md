# BUSINESS PROCESS ANALYSIS

## Current State
Status: Simulated baseline, not an observed Olist workflow.

1. Receive independent source extracts.
2. Prepare data through separate manual processes.
3. Recalculate business metrics across functions.
4. Assemble disconnected management reports.
5. Discuss performance without a unified decision framework.

## Proposed Future State
Status: Planned APEX solution.

1. Acquire and version trusted source extracts.
2. Validate data contracts and ingestion completeness.
3. Transform and reconcile warehouse tables.
4. Publish governed analytical metrics.
5. Generate visual insights and predictive outputs.
6. Evaluate potential business interventions.
7. Assign actions and measurement plans.
8. Review results and refine decisions.

## Expected Process Improvements
Greater metric consistency.
Reproducible reporting logic.
Reduced manual reconciliation effort.
Clearer ownership of analytical decisions.
More transparent evidence and uncertainty.

## Important Measurement Limitation
Improvements cannot be reported as realized without evidence from an actual implementation and measurement period.
