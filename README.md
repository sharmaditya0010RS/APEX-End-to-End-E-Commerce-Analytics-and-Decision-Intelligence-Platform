# APEX — E-Commerce Analytics & Decision Intelligence Platform

End-to-end analytics and business intelligence platform using PostgreSQL, SQL, dbt, Python, Machine Learning, Power BI, and Plotly Dash.

## Project Achievements

- 99,441 historical orders analyzed
- 96,096 unique customer identities
- R$13.59M gross merchandise value
- Six customer segments and cohort analysis
- Machine learning evaluation for delivery risk
- 162 hypothetical decision scenarios
- Five Power BI Business Impact report pages
- Five Plotly Dash dashboards with 50 interactive charts
- Responsive enterprise-white dashboard interface

## Business Intelligence

### Power BI

Five business-impact report pages covering executive performance, customer value, operational efficiency, predictive evidence, and decision scenarios.

### Plotly Dash

Five interactive analytics dashboards containing 50 charts, validated with automated browser and regression tests.

## Model and Business Impact Limitations

The delivery-risk model is not production-ready.

The financial scenario results are hypothetical and do not represent realized business savings.
